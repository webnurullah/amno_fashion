Panjabi, made modern.

Amnu Fashion is a Bangladeshi Panjabi label. We pair the comfort of everyday cotton with the finish of festive embroidery, so you look sharp at Friday prayers, at Eid gatherings and on your wedding day.

Every design starts with fabric: soft, breathable and honest. We keep our collections focused, our fits consistent and our sizing guide accurate, so ordering online feels as confident as trying it on in store.
